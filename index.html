Bio

I am a student researcher at Principia and the Gatsby Unit, UCL, supervised by Prof. Andrew Saxe and collaborating with Antonio Sclocchi and Jin Hwa Lee. My research focuses on the science of deep learning, especially how networks learn and forget. I currently use analytically tractable hierarchical and compositional data to explain phenomena observed in real LLMs. Beyond that, I am interested in the intersection of AI and neuroscience.
Previously, I completed my MSc in Machine Learning at UCL. Before that, I was an undergraduate at the School of Physics, Peking University, where I worked with Prof. Jinyi Shangguan on galaxies and supermassive black holes.

I am applying to PhD programs starting in Fall 2027!
